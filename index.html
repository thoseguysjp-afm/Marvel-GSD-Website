Welcome to the Marvel Family

At Marvel German Shepherds, we specialize in breeding the West German Show Line Shepherd. We carefully select both our German Import and Import Pedigree canines for structure, health, and sound temperament.

Our dogs are chosen for deep-pigmented Black & Red coloring in both Long Coat and Stock Coat, as well as the rare solid Black Long Coat. Pedigree, temperament, and beauty are non-negotiable at MGS.

All of our breeding dogs have hips and elbows cleared by OFA, PennHIP, or the SV in Germany, and are DNA-tested and cleared for genetic diseases common to the breed.

How to Join the Marvel Family

  1. Application — Complete our puppy application so we can understand your lifestyle and match the right temperament.
  2. Phone Interview — We contact you to discuss your needs and answer questions.
  3. Deposit — A $1,000 non-refundable deposit reserves your place (valid for two years and transferable).
  4. Matching — At 7 weeks we evaluate each puppy and match them to the best home.
  5. Go Home — Puppies leave at a minimum of 8 weeks of age with all paperwork and health records.
