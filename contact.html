Contracts & Agreements

For transparency, here is a summary of our key documents. Full signed versions are provided at the time of deposit and transfer.

  • Puppy Application — Required for all potential buyers (use the form on this site).
  • Deposit Agreement — $1,000 non-refundable deposit, transferable for two years, governed by Louisiana law (St. Tammany Parish).
  • Purchase Agreement & Health Guarantee — Includes 2-year hip/elbow guarantee (OFA/PennHIP), congenital defect coverage to 1 year, DM guarantee, limited registration terms, return policy (surrender only, no refund), and more.

If you would like PDF copies of the full Deposit Agreement or Purchase Agreement & Health Guarantee sent to you, just ask during the phone interview or via Instagram/Facebook message.

Important: We reserve the right to refuse the sale of any puppy if, in our judgment, it is not in the best interest of the dog. Under those circumstances any deposit paid is refunded in full.
