Outside Studs We Work With

We selectively stud outside the kennel to bring in complementary genetics. Current and past outside males include:

  • Esteemed K9’s Mad Max — Rare West German Showline Sable | VA1 USA Sieger Show 2024 | Normal hips/elbows | DM & MDR1 Clear
  • Mexus di Casa Caputi — IGP1, breed-surveyed (KKL) long coat male with a-normal hips and elbows. Consistent Excellent (V) ratings under USCA and SV judges.

We will continue adding high-quality outside studs as the program grows. Contact us if you have a male you would like considered for future pairings.
