About Marvel German Shepherds

At Marvel German Shepherds, we specialize in breeding the West German Show Line Shepherd. We carefully selected both our German Import and Import Pedigree Canines for our breeding program, ensuring they all meet the correct standards in structure and have a historical lineage of excellent health and sound temperament.

Our Shepherds are also selected for their deep-pigmented Black/Red coloring, in both Long Coat and Stock Coat, as well as the rare solid Black Long Coat. Assuredly, pedigree, temperament, and beauty are a must at MGS, and as such we believe our canines have the potential to produce the best representation of the breed.

As with all large breed dogs there is risk of structural degeneration. The health and structure of our canines is paramount and to ensure our due diligence any contribution to the breed, all of our canines have had their hips and elbows tested and cleared by OFA, PennHIP or the SV in Germany. Further, all our canines have been DNA tested and cleared for all genetic diseases common to GSDs.

How to Join the Marvel Family

1. Application

It is our mission to match our GSD pups with their “perfect” families. In effort to ensure that each pup finds the right “fit” in their future family, we require all potential Purchasers to complete a brief application, to determine the environment they can offer the pup as well as the best temperament of pup to suit their individual family needs.

Purchasing a puppy is a big decision and a lifetime commitment to the pup. We want to help families through this process by learning their needs, educating them on thriving with a GSD and matching them to the pup that they will connect to in an uncommon way compared to that of other dog breeds. After submitting the application, we will contact you for a brief phone interview where you can ask questions about our dogs or our mission.

2. Deposit Policy

A $1,000.00 USD deposit is required to reserve a puppy. We collect these non-refundable deposits once pregnancy is confirmed, or if Purchaser desires to wait on an upcoming pairing. If a limited number of pups is produced, we will place you on the waiting list for the next planned breeding of the same pairing or another pairing of Purchaser’s choosing.

Puppy Placement

At Marvel German Shepherds, we believe that matching the temperament of a puppy to their future owner is essential to a mutually beneficial and successful relationship. To achieve this, we use five factoring evaluations:

  1. Avidog Puppy Evaluation Test (APET)
  2. Volhard Temperament Test (both administered by a third party at 7 weeks)
  3. Careful examination of each pup’s structure and conformation assessment
  4. Qualitative assessment based on countless hours of observation and documentation of behaviors
  5. Evaluation of the Puppy Application and the Owner’s desired Puppy Options

The pups are then graded on all accounts and their temperament is determined and assigned to the Owner that best suits their needs. Puppies must be a minimum of 8 weeks of age before going to their new home.

Note: The Breeder reserves the right to first pick of both sexes. Thus, the Owner’s right to first pick can create a sliding scale in the chance the Breeder decides to keep a pup/s.

Shipping

If circumstances prevent the new owners from picking up their Marvel puppy at 8 weeks, we offer delivery services at the Purchaser’s expense. If a puppy will be flying, we recommend the Purchaser/Transporter fly with the pup in cabin. A health certificate is required to fly and will be provided by the Breeder’s veterinarian. Airline costs, health certificate and carrier for the puppy are at the buyer’s expense.

Other Puppy / Canine Options

We focus on the health and the quality of our breeding canines and the puppies they produce, never the quantity. Therefore, we only produce a few litters each year, purposefully allowing us to spend a great deal of time aiding the puppies in their early development, socialization, and care. This leads to a high demand and thus a bit of a wait for our special pups.

For this reason, Marvel German Shepherds offers a wider range of canine selections, importing both puppies and Schutzhund titled canines directly from our supplier in Europe. The process for this follows the same protocol as purchasing a Marvel Puppy, except the deposit fee is half the total cost of the imported puppy/canine and applicable veterinary and shipping fees.
